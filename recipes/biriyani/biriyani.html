Biriyani Recipe

Biriyani is a flavorful and aromatic rice dish that originated in the Indian subcontinent. It is made with basmati rice, meat (such as chicken, mutton, or beef), and a blend of spices, including saffron, cardamom, and cloves. The dish is often garnished with fried onions, fresh herbs, and served with raita or salad.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 pound meat (chicken, mutton, or beef)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/4 cup fried onions
  • Fresh herbs for garnish

Steps:

  1. Rinse the basmati rice and soak it in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Cut the meat into small pieces and marinate with spices and salt.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and add the marinated meat. Cook until done.
  4. Add the chopped onion, garlic, and ginger. Cook until the onion is translucent.
  5. Drain the soaked rice and add it to the pan. Stir well.
  6. Add water and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer until the rice is cooked.
  7. Garnish with fried onions and fresh herbs.
