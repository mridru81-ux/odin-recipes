Lasagna Recipe

Lasagna is a type of pasta made in wide, flat sheets, traditionally used to create a layered dish. The dish consists of alternating layers of pasta, ragù (a meat-based sauce), béchamel sauce, and various cheeses, such as mozzarella and Parmesan. It is typically baked in the oven until bubbly and golden.

Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps:

  1. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  3. Add the onion and garlic, and cook until the onion is translucent.
  4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  5. Simmer the sauce for 15 minutes.
  6. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese with half of the mozzarella cheese and half of the Parmesan cheese.
  7. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  8. Spread a thin layer of sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  9. Layer half of the noodles over the sauce.
  10. Spread half of the cheese mixture over the noodles.
  11. Pour half of the sauce over the cheese mixture.
  12. Repeat the layers once more.
  13. Top with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheeses.
  14. Bake for 30 minutes or until bubbly and golden.
