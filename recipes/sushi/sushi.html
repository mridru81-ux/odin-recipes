Sushi Recipe

Sushi is a traditional Japanese dish consisting of vinegared rice combined with various ingredients, such as raw fish, vegetables, and seaweed. It is often served with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 pound fresh sushi-grade fish (tuna, salmon, or yellowtail)
  • 4 sheets nori (seaweed)
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Soy sauce for serving
  • Wasabi for serving
  • Pickled ginger for serving

Steps:

  1. Rinse the sushi rice under cold water until the water runs clear.
  2. Cook the rice in a pot with water, vinegar, sugar, and salt according to package instructions.
  3. Cut the fish into thin slices.
  4. Prepare the vegetables by julienning the cucumber and slicing the avocado.
  5. Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo mat.
  6. Spread a thin layer of cooked rice on the nori, leaving a small border at the top.
  7. Add the fish slices and vegetables in a line across the center of the rice.
  8. Carefully roll the nori and rice around the fillings using the bamboo mat.
  9. Cut the roll into bite-sized pieces with a sharp knife.
